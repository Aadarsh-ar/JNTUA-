Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 16, 2026 · Latest Version

JNTUA Attendance ("we", "our", or "us") provides a mobile utility application designed to help university students view and monitor their attendance from the JNTUA student portal.

1. Information Collection & Zero-Server Policy

  • Student Credentials & Attendance Data: The application does NOT store, transmit, or share your portal passwords or personal login credentials on any external server. All attendance data and portal sessions are processed locally on your device via secure sandbox WebView.
  • Local Persistence: Attendance aggregates and historical logs are saved exclusively in your phone's sandbox storage via Expo FileSystem and AsyncStorage for offline viewing.

2. Third-Party Advertising Services

The application integrates Adsterra advertising services to sustain maintenance costs. Adsterra may process non-sensitive device identifiers and request metrics to serve advertisements in compliance with Adsterra's privacy policies.

3. Data Security

Because no central server or database is maintained by us, your educational data is mathematically impossible for us to leak or access. It remains under your complete control on your personal mobile device.

4. Open Source Transparency & Inquiries

Have questions, audit requests, or suggestions? Check our source code or contact us via our official repository:
