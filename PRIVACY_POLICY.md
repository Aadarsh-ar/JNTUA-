# Privacy Policy for JNTUA Attendance

**Effective Date:** August 16, 2026

**JNTUA Attendance** ("we", "our", or "us") provides a mobile application designed to help students check their attendance from the JNTUA student portal.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
- **Student Credentials & Data**: The app does **NOT** store, transmit, or share your portal passwords or personal login credentials on any external server. Attendance data and portal sessions are processed locally on your device via secure webview.
- **Local Storage**: Attendance summary and app state are saved locally on your device using Expo FileSystem and AsyncStorage.

### 2. Third-Party Services & Ads
The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:
- **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)**: Used to display banner, interstitial, and open-app ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve non-personalized/personalized ads according to Google's privacy policy.

### 3. Data Security
We value your trust in providing your information, and we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. Since no server database is maintained by us, your attendance data remains private on your own device.

### 4. Updates to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

### 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
